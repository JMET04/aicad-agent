# Controlled design basis — servo indexer dual-bearing flange cartridge

Status: approved for controlled AI generation and engineering review only.

## Product and interfaces

- Part ID: SIFC-220-REV-A.
- Function: locate a paired 6208 bearing set, transmit indexer radial/axial/moment reactions into a machine frame, and provide repeatable dowel location.
- Material: EN AW-7075-T651 wrought aluminium plate.
- Envelope: 220 × 180 × 56 mm maximum.
- Base: 220 × 180 × 20 mm.
- Bearing boss: Ø130 × 36 mm above the base.
- Bearing seat: Ø80 H7 × 36 mm deep for two 6208 bearings; shaft clearance Ø50 through; seal/cover recess Ø92 H8 × 6 mm.
- Indexer flange: eight Ø9 through holes on Ø108 PCD, start angle 22.5°.
- Cover flange: four Ø7 through holes on Ø104 PCD, start angle 45°.
- Frame mounting: four Ø14 through holes at X=±85, Y=±65, each with Ø24 × 10 counterbore.
- Repeatable location: two Ø8 H7 dowel holes at X=±70, Y=0.
- Stiffness features: four intersecting 42 × 72 × 12 mm integral rib pads and four 36 × 34 × 10 mm non-through lightening pockets.

## Operating authority

- Radial service load: 4.5 kN.
- Axial service load: 2.0 kN.
- Overturning moment: 350 N·m.
- Torque reaction: 500 N·m.
- Abnormal case: 2.0 g equivalent load multiplier.
- Maximum speed: 120 r/min.
- Duty: 30%; target design life: 20,000 h.
- Temperature: −10 °C to +60 °C; indoor industrial environment.
- Assembly: outer bearing rings stationary relative to housing; paired bearings retained axially by a bolted cover; frame joint uses four M12 class 10.9 bolts and two ground dowels.

## Non-negotiable intent

- Origin is the base/bearing-axis intersection; the bearing and shaft bores remain coaxial.
- Datum A remains a continuous machinable mounting plane.
- Minimum residual radial wall between Ø80 bearing seat and Ø130 boss is 25 mm.
- Minimum residual floor below lightening pockets is 10 mm.
- No mounting, dowel, flange or cover hole may intersect the bearing seat, another hole, or a pocket.
- All selectable features retain stable semantic IDs through build, save/reopen and reviewer regeneration.
