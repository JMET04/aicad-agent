# Inspection plan — SIFC-220-REV-A

Coverage: 100% of drawing characteristics are assigned a method; critical characteristics are inspected on every part during review-candidate qualification.

| Characteristic | Requirement | Method | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material/condition | 7075-T651, traceable certificate | Certificate review + positive material identification as required | Each lot |
| Datum A flatness | 0.05 mm | CMM or granite/indicator sweep | Each part |
| Bearing seat | Ø80 H7, Ra 1.6 | Air gauge or bore gauge at 20 °C + profilometer | Each part |
| Bearing seat depth | 36.00 +0.05/0.00 mm | CMM/depth gauge | Each part |
| B perpendicularity to A | 0.05 mm | CMM | Each part |
| Ø50 coaxiality to B | 0.03 mm | CMM | Each part |
| Dowel holes | 2 × Ø8 H7 and position to A|B|C | Plug gauge + CMM | Each part |
| Indexer pattern | 8 × Ø9 on Ø108 PCD, TP Ø0.10 A|B | CMM | Each part |
| Cover pattern | 4 × Ø7 on Ø104 PCD, TP Ø0.10 A|B | CMM | Each part |
| Frame mounting holes | 4 × Ø14, counterbore Ø24 × 10, TP Ø0.15 A|B|C | CMM + depth gauge | Each part |
| Residual wall/floor | boss wall ≥25; pocket floor ≥10 | CMM + model/drawing parity | First article and each setup change |
| Anodize | 25 ±5 μm, masks respected | Eddy-current thickness + visual mask audit | Each part |
| Undefined edges | 0.2–0.5 mm break, burr-free | Visual/tactile under controlled lighting | Each part |
| Part identity | SIFC-220-REV-A and revision | Traveller/marking review | Each part |

Measurement uncertainty must be ≤10% of the tolerance where practical. All dimensional results are normalized to the ISO 1 reference temperature of 20 °C or corrected and recorded.
