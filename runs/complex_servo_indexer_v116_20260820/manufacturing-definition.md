# Manufacturing definition — SIFC-220-REV-A

- Raw stock: EN AW-7075-T651 plate, minimum 230 × 190 × 60 mm, material certificate EN 10204 type 3.1 or equivalent traceable certificate.
- Primary process: CNC milling, nominal two setups plus in-process probing; datum A is established before finishing B and C.
- Heat treatment: supplied T651; no post-machining heat treatment.
- Finish: clear hard anodize 25 ±5 μm on non-fit surfaces. Mask datum A, Ø80 H7 bearing seat, Ø8 H7 dowel holes, Ø50 bore and all explicitly grounded/retained interfaces. Critical fits are inspected after all finishing.
- General size tolerance: ISO 2768-m unless individually toleranced.
- Datum/GPS: A bottom face; B Ø80 bearing-seat axis; C primary Ø8 H7 dowel axis. A flatness 0.05; B perpendicularity 0.05 to A; Ø50 coaxiality 0.03 to B; both bolt patterns true position Ø0.10 to A|B; frame holes true position Ø0.15 to A|B|C.
- Surface texture: Ø80 H7 and dowel holes Ra 1.6; datum A Ra 3.2; other machined surfaces Ra 6.3 unless noted.
- Undefined edges: ISO 13715; break sharp edges 0.2–0.5 mm and deburr. No native CAD fillet/chamfer is relied upon.
- Fasteners: frame interface uses four ISO metric M12 class 10.9 bolts, validated washer/nut stack and controlled lubricant; target torque is 118 N·m pending process confirmation.
- Standard parts/BOM closure for this part-level drawing: item 1 SIFC-220-REV-A, quantity 1; mating 6208 bearings, cover and M12 hardware are interface references, not included supply.
- Fixturing: full datum-A support in setup 1; soft jaws or expanding arbor on finished Ø80 reference in setup 2 without clamping distortion.
- Tool access: Ø24 counterbores accept standard M12 socket-head tooling; all pattern holes are normal to A and accessible from the boss side.
- Critical-process targets: Cpk ≥1.33 for Ø80 H7, dowel spacing/position and A-to-B perpendicularity after process qualification.
